BULK TACTICS

Eat on a Clock

Set alarms at 7am, noon, 5pm, 9pm. Your appetite won't tell you to eat enough — the schedule does. Non-negotiable.

Drink Whole Milk Constantly

Whole milk is the cheapest calorie-dense liquid on earth. Carry it. Drink it with every meal. It adds 300+ calories per day with zero effort.

Night Mass Shake is Sacred

Never skip Meal 4. This is your easiest 680 calories. Blend the oats — don't chew them. It goes down in 3 minutes flat.

Rice is Your Backbone

Cook 6-8 cups of rice every 2 days and store in the fridge. Rice is cheap, dense, and goes with everything. Never run out.

Fat is Free Calories

Butter on everything. Don't drain your beef grease — cook in it. Add olive oil to pasta before plating. These small adds are 200+ extra calories per day.

Weeks 1-2 Will Be Rough

You will feel too full. Push through it anyway. Your stomach will expand and hunger signals will catch up around week 3. Don't reduce portions early.

Weigh Yourself Weekly

Same time, same conditions — first thing Monday morning after using the bathroom. You should be up 2-2.5 lbs/week. If not, add calories.

Lift or the Surplus Becomes Fat

You want to get fat but not only fat. Compound lifts 3-4x/week tells your body to partition surplus into muscle too. Keep training hard.

Calorie Escalation

Weeks 1-3: hit 3,400. Weeks 4-7: push to 3,600. Weeks 8-10: push to 3,800+ if stomach allows. Your metabolism will adapt — keep ahead of it.
